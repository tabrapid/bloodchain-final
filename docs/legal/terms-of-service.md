## 1. The service

The Bloodchain donor app ("the app") is provided by **[legal name]** ("we")
to let registered blood donors book donation and laboratory appointments,
respond to emergency blood requests, view results published to them, and take
part in recognition features. Couriers use a separate workspace in the same
app to carry deliveries assigned to them.

## 2. Your account

You must give accurate information when you register and keep your sign-in
details to yourself. One person, one account. You can close your account from
Privacy → Delete account; what that does is written on that screen and in the
Privacy Policy.

## 3. Medical information

Blood group, eligibility and laboratory results shown in the app come from
the blood service and the laboratories, not from the app. Nothing in the app
is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. "AI insights", where enabled, are
informational text generated from your own results and are labelled as such.
Decisions about whether you may donate are made by the blood service's
staff at the time of donation.

## 4. Appointments and emergencies

Booking an appointment is a commitment to attend or to cancel in the app.
Accepting an emergency request is a commitment to travel to the named
hospital within the stated time; the hospital confirms your visit when you
accept. You can withdraw your response in the app. The app does not provide
navigation or estimated arrival times.

## 5. Acceptable use

Do not use the app to impersonate anyone, to submit false health
information, to access another person's data, or to interfere with the
service. We may suspend an account used in those ways.
